These files are adapted from agent-browser/mods/agent-browser in deevs-herdr-kit
(https://github.com/DeevsDeevs/deevs-herdr-kit):

- discovery.js, from lib.js: the session discovery script, its parser and
  the address shortener.
- page.js, from lib.js: the address normalizer, the JPEG size reader, the
  page mapping, and the mouse and keyboard messages. Its tunnel argument
  list comes from connect.js.
- stream.js, from stream.js: the stream connection, frame pacing and input
  queue.
- frames.js, from frames.js: the frame files that make the live picture.
- connect.js, from connect.js: the direct connection and the ssh tunnel.
